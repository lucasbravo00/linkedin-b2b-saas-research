## How these experts were selected

The brief explicitly warns against "the first Google results." So the bar here was:

1. **Practitioner, not commentator.** Every person on this list grows *their own* B2B audience on LinkedIn (or runs a business that does it for others) and can point to outcomes — followers, pipeline, revenue, or a documented method. Pure "thought-leadership-about-thought-leadership" voices were cut.
2. **On-topic for B2B SaaS.** General "personal branding" or "post more on LinkedIn" influencers were deprioritized in favor of people working specifically with B2B software companies and founders.
3. **Currently active (verified June 2026).** Each profile/feed was checked for recent activity. One well-known name (Chris Walker) was *deliberately excluded* because he exited his B2B agency in 2025 and his recent content is now off-topic — see "Considered but excluded" at the bottom.
4. **Complementary angles.** The ten are chosen to *together* cover everything a real playbook needs: strategy, the content engine/system, founder-led execution, writing craft, distribution, and the underlying algorithm data — not ten people saying the same thing.

> Profile URLs were verified via web research in June 2026. LinkedIn occasionally changes vanity slugs — confirm each handle before pointing an automated collector at it.
