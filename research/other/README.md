# Other materials

Anything that isn't a LinkedIn post or a YouTube transcript but supports the playbook:

- **Reports / data** — e.g. Richard van der Blom's *Algorithm Insights Report 2025*
  (download: https://sales.richardvanderblom.com/content-algorithm-playbook/)
- **Newsletters** — MKT1 (Emily Kramer), The Reeder (Devin Reed), Wes Kao's newsletter,
  The Workflow (Sara Stella Lattanzio)
- **Podcasts** — Exit Five (Gerhardt), The Founder-Led Marketing Show (Thormeier),
  Reed Between the Lines (Reed), Branded (Sordell)
- **Screenshots** — high-performing posts where layout/formatting matters (carousels, hooks)

Suggested convention: one markdown file per item with a link, date, 2–3 sentence
summary in your own words, and why it matters. Keep quotes short and always attribute.
