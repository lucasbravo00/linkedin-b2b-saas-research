## Key Findings Summary

Based on analysis of ~1.8M posts across 58k profiles and 31k company pages, van der Blom's 2025 Algorithm Insights Report documents a stark shift in LinkedIn's organic reach landscape: organic reach has dropped ~50%, engagement is down 25%, and follower growth has declined 59% year-over-year. The algorithmic wins now favor **native formats over links**—document carousels (PDFs) achieve 6.60% engagement rates (highest of any format), while a single external link in the post body reduces median reach by 18.8%. The first 60–90 minutes after posting are decisive: early engagement (comments, shares, dwell time) signals to the algorithm whether to amplify or suppress the post, making initial momentum a primary driver of reach trajectory.

## Why This Matters for the Playbook

The playbook must acknowledge that **volume and vanity metrics no longer scale**. Founders and content teams chasing impressions without strategic positioning will post into a low-reach environment; instead, the playbook must teach *deliberate format choice* (prefer documents/carousels), *authentic engagement strategies* (cold-start within your network in the first hour), and *dwell-time thinking* (write for clarity and re-reads, not just scroll-stops). Van der Blom's data arms practitioners with the "why" behind each tactic—separating hype from evidence—essential for a playbook that claims to be grounded in real B2B outcomes.
