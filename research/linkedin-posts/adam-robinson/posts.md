## Post 3 — 2026-06-06
**Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/retentionadam_what-actually-is-an-autonomous-business-activity-7469089758158163968-kgi_
**Why it matters:** Redefinir un buzzword ('autonomous business') + POV propio + reveal de métricas (7%/mo, ~$10m ARR, 90 min/semana). Posiciona el thought leadership como canal de adquisición barato.

> What actually IS an autonomous business? I started noticing the term 'autonomous business' when Amos Bar-Joseph entered RB2B's space and made a splash with an incredibly compelling narrative about AI and building the startup of the future. I remember reading posts from 2 years ago like it was yesterday… He had this exciting way of positioning himself as a true AI expert, creating a totally new type of startup. 'Just three guys and an army of 30 AI agents', 'scaling to $30m ARR' (my favorite credibility line ever). I - along with thousands of others - read these posts and WANTED all of these agents. But I also had this feeling that the way they were going about it, Swan AI wasn't ever going to be truly autonomous … Nor was that the goal. These guys were actually working around the clock with these agents, and planned to continue to do so indefinitely. And by the way - there is nothing wrong with that. In fact, I'd go as far as to say if you are NOT committed to working around the clock indefinitely, you will almost certainly NOT be successful. But back to my original question … Is 3 guys working around the clock ACTUALLY an autonomous business? My answer would be 'no'. I have a fairly un-evolved definition of an 'autonomous business' … My definition is 'a business that runs itself without any humans.' Ironically, when we started RB2B, that was the ultimate goal, but never became part of the story. We designed the entire machine to be simple enough to where all customer interaction could be handled by AI, and our cheap acquisition channel (my thought leadership) helped us price so low that people didn't expect more than that from us. Fast forward 2 years... We refuse to add anything beyond a pixel and a push, AI handles over 99% of our total customer tickets, and our CSAT is in the high 90's. What's even crazier is that we stopped working on RB2B six weeks ago to pursue MoltSets. What do I mean by that? - Tate has not touched the code in six weeks (true story) - Robb spends under 15min/day answering AI-escalated tickets - I post 3x/week on LinkedIn, ironically NOT about RB2B… And we're currently growing at over 7%/mo, will cross $10m ARR soon, and it's taking under 90min of human time per week. Based on my primitive definition - a business that runs itself w/o humans - RB2B is the closest thing to 'autonomous' that I've ever heard of. How would you define an autonomous business? What are other examples?

> In 2017, a 45 minute podcast interview with Ross Andrew Paquette, where he said he bootstrapped to $25M ARR with only ONE sales rep, changed the trajectory of my life forever. BACKGROUND Have you ever heard of Roger Banister? In 1954, Roger Banister was the first human to run a mile under 4min. For 1000's of years, people believed that a human could not break 4min. Here's the crazy thing… By the end of 1955, 24 people had broken the 4min mile. Why? Because THEY NOW KNEW it was possible. This is the story of my Roger Banister moment. ACT 1 / ROBLY EMAIL MARKETING In 2014 I launched an Email Marketing app called Robly. We got stuck at $3m in 2016, and remained stuck until November 2019. ACT 2 / THE PODCAST Throughout the Robly journey, I was searching for any information that might get us unstuck. That's when I came across the podcast with Ross and learned he bootstrapped $25m ARR with ONE SALES REP! Insane! ACT 3 / THE GOAL Now I knew it was possible. And even though I was stuck at $235k MRR, $2MM MRR became my goal. I wrote in my journal that I would hit it by my 42nd birthday. ACT 4 / DIANA ROSSI I HAD to find out what Ross knew about the Email Marketing space that I didn't. So I hit up his old salespeople. That led to chatting with Diana Ross, who had left Maropost. We spoke more and more over the course of the year, and in 2018 decided to start a business together. ACT 5 / THE EPIC FAIL We spent a year building a new newsletter app, and our plan was to 'launch' at the Traffic & Conversions summit. I had to figure out how to salvage it. I remembered hearing it was possible to have an anonymous visitor hit a website and you could resolve it to an email address. We didn't get to $25m with one rep, but… We DID get to $12m ARR with 6 people and only ONE REP! TAKEAWAY My life changed when I heard Ross got Maropost to $25m ARR with one rep. Startups can get WAY farther than you think, with FAR fewer resources. BUT... You must have YOUR OWN Roger Banister moment. YOU must realize what's possible. That's why this Tuesday, I'm sitting down LIVE with Ross Paquette himself.
